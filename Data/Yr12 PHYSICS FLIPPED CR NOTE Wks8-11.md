Laws of Electromagnetic Induction
There are two laws of electromagnetic induction: 1. Faraday’s
Law 2. Lenz’s Law.
1. Faraday’s Law of Electromagnetic induction states that
whenever there is a change in the magnetic flux of linking
a coil or circuit, an emf is induced, which is proportional to
the rate of change of magnetic flux linking the coil or
circuit.
E α dΦ/dt, E = -KdΦ/dt, if only k = 1 , E = -dΦ/dt, the
equation can be written as E = -NdΦ/dt
N = Number of turns, dΦ/dt, = rate of change of magnetic

flux in one turn. The unit is weber/s. The magnetic flux
linking the coils depends on:
The magnetic field strength

The number of turns in the coil.

The area of each turn.

Faradays law gives the magnitude of the induced e.m.f.
2. Lenz’s Law of electromagnetic induction states that the
direction of induced emf/current is such as to oppose the
motion/change producing it.
Thus Lenz law gives the direction of the induced current
or induced emf.
Lenz’s law is an application of the law of conservation of
energy, there is a transformation of mechanical energy to
Electrical Energy (induced current). Energy is expended
when the induced current flows and the source of this
energy is the work done when the magnet is moved.

THE INDUCTION COIL: This is an electrical device capable of
producing a very high intermittent e.m.f. by electromagnetic
induction from a low voltage d.c. source e.g. a battery.
Structure: It consists of:
A primary coil, made up of a few turns of insulated thick

copper wire, wound on a laminated soft iron
Secondary coil overlapping the primary coil. It is made of a

large number of turns of insulated thin copper wire. The
ends of this coil are connected to an adjustable spark gap
created by one fixed and one adjustable brass rods.
In front of the soft iron core of the primary coil is a make-

and-break-device consisting of a soft iron armature carried
on a light brass spring to which is attached a platinium
contact with adjustable screw.
The primary circuit is completed with a key and battery.

Principle of Operation
When the key is closed/press, the core becomes

magnetized due to the inflow of current through the

primary coil.
The soft iron armature is attracted by the magnetized core,

thereby causing a break in the circuit at the contact point.
As a result, the magnetic field through the core and the
primary coil collapses rapidly.
By means of spring, the armature flies back and remakes

contact with the adjustable screw again due to
demagnetization of the core.
The circuit is completed again and the process is repeated

rapidly.
This action causes rapid changes in the magnetic flux

through the primary coil, which induces a rapidly changing
e.m.f. (a.c. e.m.f.) in the secondary coil
Because the number of turns in the secondary coil is far

greater than those in the primary coil, a large e.m.f., large
enough to cause a spark across the gap is produced in the
secondary coil.
The capacitor (C) is connected between the armature and the
adjustable screw because;
i. To control/reduce sparking at the platinum contacts
(which otherwise wears off the contact point) due to the
back e.m.f. generated in the primary coil.
ii. It promotes rapid decay of the magnetic flux at the break
of the primary circuit, which helps to increase the
secondary e.m.f.
Uses
It is used in;
i. Motor car ignition system
ii. The operation of x-ray tubes
iii.The investigate of high voltages
iv.The study of electric discharge through gases
v. Merse radio transmitter.
