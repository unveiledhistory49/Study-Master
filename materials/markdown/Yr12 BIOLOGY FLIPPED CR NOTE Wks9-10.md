GENETICS: (BIOLOGY OF HEREDITY)
Genetics is definedas thescientificstudyof heredityandvariationinlivingorganisms.While
Heredity(Inheritance) is thetransmissionandexpressionof traits or characters from parents to
offspringviagenes.During meiosis, gametes are formedfrom homologous chromosomes andthey
containhalf thenumber of chromosomes (n-number) inasomaticcell.Eachgamete thus contains
onlyone copyof agene that controls aparticular character.During fertilization, haploidgametes from
parents fuseto form adiploid(2n) zygotei.e.thereare two copies of acharacter inheritedbythe
offspring.Variation is the differencethat exist betweenagene, parent andoffspringor among
members of apopulation.

Eachchromosomeconsists of two chromatids heldtogether at centromere.After delldivision,
chromatids becomelongthread-likestructures (about 0.2um-50um) wrappedaroundhistones to
form Chromatin Fibres.Eachchromatinfibreis madeupof 60%proteinand40%DNA.DNAconsists
of atwo helicalchains formedfrom repeatednucleotides.Eachnucleotideis composedof a
Deoxyribose/Ribosesugar(Pentose), aphosphategroupandanitrogenous basewhichmaybe
Adenine, Thymine, Cytosine orGuanine.Therepeatednucleotides arearrangedinsteps of aladder.
Eachstepof ladder has two bases joinedbyahydrogenbondinthis format ‘Adenine always bond
withThymine andCytosine withGuanine/ A=T and C=G’.The two helicalstrands arealso knownas
Complementarystrands of DNAsinceoneis theexact oppositeof theother.Thesequenceof triplets
of bases/codoninDNAdetermines thesequenceof amino acids inapeptidee.g. AAT- Leucine, CGA
-Alanine, CCA -Glycine, etc. Andpeptides determinethe complex polypeptide/protein(Genetic
coding).For example, Insulin is animal hormone with 51 amino acids i.e. 153 nitrogenous bases.
Inagivenspecies, thereare2 types of chromosome i.e. Autosomes (pairs that arealike inshapeand
size) and Sex chromosomes (pair that differ intheir sexes). Thesomaticcells inhuman possess
22pairs of autosome and1 pair of sex chromosome i.e. 46(23pairs), cow-60, dog-78, monkey-54,
chimpanzee-48, potato-48, roundworm-2, lion-38, mosquito-6, peaplant-14, etc.
